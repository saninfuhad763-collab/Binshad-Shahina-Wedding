## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an atmospheric, black-tie editorial digital salon. Moving far beyond traditional, pastel wedding websites, it draws from avant-garde fashion lookbooks, cinematic title cards, and private cultural clubs. The aesthetic balances intense intimacy with architectural restraint.

The target audience comprises discerning couples and high-end guests who appreciate fine typography, couture tailoring, and archival print culture. The experience feels like unwrapping an engraved velvet folio in dim candlelight. 

Key style tenets include:
- **Cinematic Pacing:** Generous negative space, deliberate chapter breaks, and calculated visual pauses.
- **Curated Contrast:** Severe, near-black wine tones punctuated by luminous champagne accents and sharp editorial typography.
- **Understated Materiality:** Fine hairline rules, subdued tinted glows, and precise typographic tracking rather than heavy shadows or kitsch ornamentation.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an obsessive study of vintage Bordeaux wines, aged parchment, and heirloom metalwork. 

- **Primary (`#4A101D`):** Deep imperial burgundy. Used for focal anchor points, active states, key interactive indicators, and dramatic backdrop washes.
- **Secondary (`#B89A72`):** Muted bullion gold. Reserved for fine hairline details, monograms, subtle indicators, and bespoke accents.
- **Tertiary (`#65182A`):** Vivid wine velvet. Deployed for subtle state transitions, selected card borders, and warm gradients against the dark base.
- **Neutral Palette:**
  - Base Obsidian: `#080606` (page foundation)
  - Surface Noir: `#0D090A` (structural containers)
  - Elevated Plum-Noir: `#240B12` (floating surfaces and cards)
  - Muted Champagne: `#E8D8C5` (secondary typography and subdued text)
  - Soft Warm White: `#F5EFE8` (primary typography, sharp contrast)
  - Hairline Rule Tint: `rgba(232, 216, 197, 0.12)`

## Typography

The typography operates on sharp theatrical contrast:

- **Playfair Display** introduces romantic gravitas, poetic weight, and human warmth. It must be set with relaxed line heights for body quotes and tightly kerned tracks for massive display headers. It represents the timeless tradition of the event.
- **Manrope** introduces pristine architectural clarity, legibility, and modern detachment. Used for body text, interactive controls, metadata, and directional markers.
- **Couture Micro-Tracking:** All caps labels (`label-caps`, `label-md`) strictly employ aggressive tracking (`0.08em` to `0.24em`), echoing luxury atelier invoices and gallery collection titles.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is conceived as a sequence of gallery vignettes and bound printed spreads. 

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column asymmetric grid for desktop (`max-width: 1440px`), collapsing to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. Elements freely utilize asymmetrical column offsets (e.g., body copy spanning columns 3–8 with blank negative flanks) to evoke editorial magazines.
- **Whitespace Discipline:** Spacing intervals are deliberate. Sections rely on `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` gaps to instill patience, rhythm, and anticipation before major revelations (e.g., date, venue, RSVP).
- **Responsive Handling:** Margins fluidly contract from desktop's monumental `margin` (4rem) down to mobile `margin-mobile` (1.25rem), while headline scales step down gracefully to prevent awkward word wrapping.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system rejects aggressive, generic drop shadows. Instead, it relies on tonal layering, calibrated obsidian transparency, and subtle incandescent glows reminiscent of low-lit architectural lighting:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#080606` deep matte obsidian base.
  - **Level 1 (Panels & Frames):** `#0D090A` with a 1px perimeter border rendered in `rgba(232, 216, 197, 0.08)`.
  - **Level 2 (Modals & Overlays):** `#240B12` semi-opaque fill blended with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and an inner 1px border of `rgba(184, 154, 114, 0.2)`.
- **Atmospheric Glow:** Interactive focal cards receive a diffuse ambient aura: `box-shadow: 0 20px 50px -10px rgba(74, 16, 29, 0.45), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(184, 154, 114, 0.15)`.
- **Depth Transitions:** Elevations do not simply translate upward on the Z-axis; their border luminances shift subtly from muted champagne to bullion gold upon hover.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a refined, tailor-cut geometry:

- **Border Radius:** Locked to `roundedness: 1` (`0.25rem` / `4px` baseline). Buttons, inputs, and image frames maintain crisp, nearly architectural corners, nodding to deckled heavy-card invitations and bevel-cut passe-partout matting.
- **Large Cards & Modals:** Use `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / `8px`) maximum. High curvature is strictly forbidden as it undermines the formal, black-tie tenor of the experience.
- **Dividers & Embellishments:** Structural dividers are strictly 1px hairlines styled with gradient masks fading out at each terminal edge.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Links
- **Primary Action (The Gold Standard):** Background in `#4A101D`, text in `#F5EFE8`, with an outer hairline border in `#B89A72`. Padded with `space-md` horizontally, `space-sm` vertically. Font set to `label-md`. Hover state transforms the background to `#65182A` and brightens the gold rim.
- **Secondary / Ghost Action:** Transparent background with a delicate border (`rgba(232, 216, 197, 0.2)`), text in `#E8D8C5`. Hover state fills with `rgba(74, 16, 29, 0.25)` and sets border to `#B89A72`.
- **Editorial Text Link:** Inline text in `#E8D8C5` featuring a bespoke 1px underline situated 4px beneath the baseline, expanding outward on hover from the center with a transition to `#F5EFE8`.

### Input Fields & RSVP Controls
- **Form Fields:** Grounded in a transparent fill with a single bottom hairline border (`rgba(232, 216, 197, 0.25)`). Text aligns to `body-md` in `#F5EFE8`.
- **Focus State:** Bottom border lights up to solid `#B89A72` accompanied by a soft, warm wine background tint (`rgba(74, 16, 29, 0.12)`). Labels rest in `label-caps` in `#B89A72`.
- **Dietary & Guest Selection Chips:** Minimalist low-contrast capsules. Selected state: background `#4A101D`, border `#B89A72`, text `#F5EFE8`. Unselected state: background `#0D090A`, border `rgba(232, 216, 197, 0.12)`, text `#E8D8C5`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square forms (`4px` radius) with 1px border of `#B89A72`.
- Active state displays an interior gold monogram diamond tick or fill rather than a standard system checkmark.

### Cards & Keepsake Portfolios
- **Editorial Keepsake Card:** Base of `#0D090A` surrounded by a dual hairline frame (1px outer border, 4px inset margin, 1px inner faint border). Photography displayed within uses subtle desaturation and warm tinting, lifting to full vivid color on scroll or cursor contact.
- **Event Timeline / Chapter Blocks:** Left-anchored with continuous vertical hairline rules. Time markers displayed in `label-caps` (`#B89A72`), event titles rendered in `headline-sm` (`#F5EFE8`), and location descriptions set in `body-md` (`#E8D8C5`).

### Bespoke System Additions
- **Monogram Seal:** A scalable vector lockup combining initials with delicate astrological or classical floral linework, finished in brushed gold `#B89A72`.
- **Audio Scrobbler / Ambient Score Toggle:** A discreet floating pill in the bottom corner with a living, pulsing waveform in `#B89A72` over `#240B12` glassmorphism, controlling the celebration's curated soundtrack.
